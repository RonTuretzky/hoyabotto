# parts/ -- 3D-printed fixtures for the XLeRobot micro-farm

Generated by `parts/make_parts.py` (pure numpy binary-STL writer, no trimesh /
numpy-stl).  From `software/` with the venv active:

```
python parts/make_parts.py      # writes parts/out/*.stl, prints bbox + triangle table
```

Every part is a union of simple closed shells (boxes, square-with-round-hole
cells, a tapered ring).  Shells overlap by 0.02 mm on purpose so the slicer
fuses them; all features are steps whose upper faces sit on material, so no
part has an overhang and none needs supports.  Parts are modelled in print
orientation (flat face on the bed, Z up).  All dimensions are parameters at
the top of the script.

Target printer: AnkerMake M5C, 220 x 220 x 250 mm bed, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA.

## Parts

### `nest_cress.stl` -- locating nest for the "Macce" cress wicking trough
Keeps the trough in a fixed, repeatable pose on the table for the SO-101 arms.

* Base plate 200 x 105 x 4 mm with a 6 mm rim (rim top at Z = 10).
* Pocket 181.5 x 86.5 mm (0.75 mm clearance per side around a 180 x 85 trough);
  3 mm of vertical wall, then a stepped 45-degree lead-in (3 ledges, 1 mm rise /
  1 mm inset), so the opening at the rim top is 187.5 x 92.5 mm.
* 30 mm wide refill / finger notch through the rim on the -X short side
  (rim removed down to the base plate) for a bottle spout or a fingertip.
* Front apron (-Y) 200 x 48 mm carrying a 40.5 x 40.5 x 1.2 mm AprilTag
  recess (0.25 mm clearance per side for the 40 mm tag tiles below).  With a
  tile inserted, the tile's white surface sits 0.2 mm below the plate and the
  raised black cells stand 0.4 mm above it.
* Four M4 (4.5 mm) fixing holes: two in 16 x 12 mm tabs on the rear (+Y)
  corners, two in the front apron corners.  Hole centres at
  (8, 111), (192, 111), (8, -40), (192, -40) with the trough pocket origin at (0, 0).
* Overall 200 x 165 x 10 mm -- fits the 220 x 220 bed in either orientation.

### `tag_36h11_id1.stl`, `tag_36h11_id2.stl` -- AprilTag tiles
40 x 40 x 1.0 mm tiles, 8 x 8 cells of 5 mm (6 x 6 data bits plus the black
border), black cells raised 0.6 mm (3 layers at 0.2 mm).  Total height 1.6 mm.

The bit patterns are **not guessed**.  They were taken from the official
images `tag36h11/tag36_11_00001.png` and `tag36_11_00002.png` in
<https://github.com/AprilRobotics/apriltag-imgs> (10 x 10 px, outer 1 px white
border stripped), decoded with a pure-python PNG reader, and cross-checked
against the code words in `tag36h11.c` of <https://github.com/AprilRobotics/apriltag>
(`codedata[1] = 0x0000000dda664ca7`, `codedata[2] = 0x0000000dc4a1c821`) laid
out with that file's `bit_x` / `bit_y` tables.  Both derivations agree exactly;
the patterns are embedded as 8 x 8 strings in the script.  Row 0 of the image
is at +Y on the tile.

Making the raised cells actually black (choose one):
* Print in white/light PLA and add a filament change (M600 / slicer colour
  change) at Z = 1.0 mm to black PLA.  This is the clean option.
* Print in white PLA and paint the raised cells with a black marker.
* Or skip the tiles and print the two tags on paper (from apriltag-imgs, scaled
  to 40 mm outer black square) and glue them into the recess.

Detection is rotation-invariant; for consistent pose frames, place the tile
with its +Y (image top) toward the trough.

### `paddle_bh1750.stl` -- light-sensor paddle for a parallel gripper
* Handle 120 x 18 x 8 mm.  Two 2 mm wide, 1 mm deep grip grooves across the
  handle (top and bottom faces) at 25 and 45 mm from the gripper end.
* 30 x 24 mm platform (same 8 mm thickness) with a 20.5 x 16 x 3 mm pocket for
  a 19.5 x 15 mm BH1750 breakout (0.5 mm clearance per side).
* 4 mm wide, 2.5 mm deep cable channel on the top centre-line from the pocket
  to the gripper end.
* Two 2.2 mm holes (M2 clearance) through the pocket floor, 14.5 mm apart along
  the handle axis, on the pocket centre-line -- **assumed spacing, verify**.
* Overall 150 x 24 x 8 mm.  The bottom grooves are 2 mm bridges over a 1 mm
  slot; PLA bridges this trivially.

### `bottle_rest.stl` -- cup rest for the refill bottle
* Base 70 x 70 x 4 mm, ring 8 mm tall (top at Z = 12), inner diameter 56 mm
  (54 mm bottle + 1 mm clearance per side; `BOTTLE_D` / `BOTTLE_CLEAR` are
  parameters), 4 mm wall (OD 64).
* 40-degree lead-in taper on the top 2.5 mm of the inner wall (inner radius
  opens from 28.0 to 30.1 mm; wall still 1.9 mm thick at the top).  The cone
  faces up/inward, so no overhang.
* Two M4 (4.5 mm) holes at opposite corners, centres (7, 7) and (63, 63).

## Print settings
* 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20 % infill (gyroid or grid), 4 top / 4 bottom layers.
* PLA, 210-220 C nozzle, 60 C bed.  No supports needed for any part.
* Tags: print at 0.2 mm; the 0.6 mm relief is exactly 3 layers.  Colour change
  at Z = 1.0 mm if printing two-colour.
* Nest: 200 x 165 mm footprint -- use a brim if your bed adhesion is marginal;
  slow the first layer.
* Print all parts as exported (flat face down).  Do not scale.

## Dimensional assumptions to verify against the real parts
| # | Assumption | Where used | If wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Cress trough footprint is a plain rectangle 180 x 85 mm at its base, 25 mm tall, with vertical or slightly drafted sides and no flared lip wider than 187.5 x 92.5 mm | `TROUGH_L/W`, pocket | change `TROUGH_L/W` or `POCKET_CLEAR`; a flared lip needs a taller rim or bigger lead-in |
| A2 | 0.75 mm per-side clearance is right for your printer's XY tolerance | `POCKET_CLEAR` | measure the printed pocket; typical PLA XY error is +/-0.1-0.2 mm |
| A3 | AprilTag tile clearance 0.25 mm per side | `TAG_RECESS_CLEAR` | tile too tight -> increase; too loose -> use a dab of glue |
| A4 | Table fixing with M4 screws, 4.5 mm clearance holes | `M4_HOLE_D` | change for wood screws (e.g. 3.5 mm) |
| A5 | BH1750 breakout is 19.5 x 15 mm (GY-302 style) | `BH_PCB_L/W`, pocket | measure and set; pocket = PCB + 2 x 0.5 mm |
| A6 | **BH1750 mounting-hole spacing 14.5 mm along the long axis, centred on the short axis, M2 (2.2 mm) holes** | `BH_HOLE_SPACING`, `BH_HOLE_D` | measure your module (many GY-302 boards have only two holes near one edge, some have 3 mm holes); edit and regenerate |
| A7 | Cable exits the pocket toward the gripper end; 4 mm channel fits the wires/connector | `CABLE_W/D` | widen or deepen; channel depth is limited by the 8 mm handle |
| A8 | Parallel gripper clamps the 18 x 8 mm handle within 25-45 mm of the end; 1 mm grooves are enough grip | `GRIP_GROOVE_*` | move grooves to match the jaw pads |
| A9 | Bottle diameter 52-55 mm; ID 56 gives 0.5-2 mm of play | `BOTTLE_D`, `BOTTLE_CLEAR` | set `BOTTLE_D` to the measured diameter |
| A10 | 3 mm of vertical pocket wall (before the lead-in steps) locates a 25 mm tall trough adequately | `RIM_H`, `LEDGE_STEPS` | raise `RIM_H` for a taller rim |

## Germination kit (HomesteadAce square tray, 220 x 220 x 50 mm) -- no nest
The tray is exactly the bed size; a nest with any rim or clearance would be
>= 224 mm and does not fit the M5C bed with margin, so no nest is generated
for it.  Options:
* Use only the cress planter for the robot workflow (recommended for now).
* Print corner locators instead of a full nest: four small L-brackets screwed
  to the table at the tray's corners give the same repeatability and are tiny
  prints.  (Not included; can be added to `make_parts.py` on request.)
* Scaling a nest to 95 % would only make sense for a ~209 mm tray -- a scaled
  nest does not fit the real 220 mm tray, so we do not recommend that route.
