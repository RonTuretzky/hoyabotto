1. Trust the local camera connection

Your browser needs a secure connection to use the camera. Install this temporary certificate once on this phone.

iPhone or iPad · open in Safari
  1. Tap Download iPhone certificate below and allow the download.
  2. Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Select Robot Camera Local, tap Install, and follow the phone’s prompts.
  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Enable full trust for Robot Camera Local.
  4. Return to this page in Safari.
Download iPhone certificate
Android · open in Chrome
  1. Tap Download Android certificate below.
  2. In Settings, search for Install a certificate. Choose CA certificate and select camera-ca.cer. Settings names vary by phone.
  3. Return to this page in Chrome.
Download Android certificate

This is a certificate-only profile, with no device management. Trusting a root certificate is a phone security setting. This certificate expires after seven days; its signing key was discarded after creating this Mac’s certificate. Remove it from your phone when finished.

2. Open the camera

Allow camera access, then tap Start camera. Aim at the whole robot, including both arms and their cables.

Open secure camera

Keep that page open and your screen awake. If you see a certificate warning, finish the trust steps above; don’t bypass the warning. If the page cannot connect, check that the phone and Mac are on the same Wi-Fi and that VPN or guest-network isolation is not blocking local connections.
