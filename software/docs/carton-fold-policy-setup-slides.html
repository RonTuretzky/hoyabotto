Carton fold policy · station setup

Set up the box for the learned two-flap fold

A step-by-step guide to laying out the robot, table, carton and cameras so the simulation-trained policy sees what it was trained on, then to running it safely for the first time.

Before you start

Safety first, then gather the kit

Safety rules

  • Someone holds STOP (robot_stop) whenever a motor is enabled.
  • The 12 V switch is within reach. Switch it off before touching any servo wire.
  • Nobody inside the arms' sweep while they move.
  • First motion runs use no carton, free air, 10 steps.
  • Never relax limits in farm/safety/rules.py to make it work.

What you need

  • Tape measure (mm) and painter's tape
  • Phone with an inclinometer app
  • An empty carton, 379 × 283 × 108 mm, 140 mm flaps
  • A flat platform or table you can set to the right height
  • Four printed AprilTags (tag36h11, 60–100 mm): tools/carton_tags_pdf.py
  • The robot: both SO101 arms, head OAK-D Lite, both wrist cameras

Step 1 · do this first

Measure the arm spacing

Owner5 min
  1. Find the shoulder-pan axis of each arm: the centre of the round plate that turns, above the base servo.
  2. Tape-measure between the two centres.
  3. Photograph the tape.
Go / no-go

The policy was trained with the arm-base origins 220 mm apart, as in the robot model. The model turns its bases sideways, which would put the pan axes about 310 mm apart instead.

Whatever you measure, send it. If it isn't 220 mm, new demonstrations get recorded at your number before training: a few hours of compute, not days.

Step 1 · arm spacing
Step 2

Set the carton surface 120 mm below the arm bases

Owner15–30 min
  1. Measure from the platform top up to the underside of each SO101 base.
  2. Raise or lower the platform (boards, books, a box) until both arms read 120 mm.
  3. Left and right must agree within 3 mm. If not, the cart or platform is tilted: shim it.
  4. If the base underside is hidden, measure floor → base and floor → platform, then subtract.
Step 2 · height
Step 3

Square the cart, then tape the carton's spot

Owner15 min

Cart to table edge

  1. From each shoulder-pan axis, measure straight out to the near table edge.
  2. Move the cart until both sides read 111 mm. That is 150 mm from the base line; the pan axis sits 39 mm in front of it.
  3. If left and right differ, the cart is turned. Rotate it until they match.

Carton position

  1. Near wall of the carton 10 mm back from the table edge.
  2. Carton centre 10 mm to the robot's left of the midpoint between the arms (anywhere from 25 mm left to 5 mm right works).
  3. Square to the table edge, within 4°.
  4. Tape around the carton's footprint, so every run starts in the same place.
Step 3 · setback and carton spot
Step 4

Prepare the carton

Owner5 min
  1. Use an empty carton. Check it is 379 × 283 × 108 mm with 140 mm flaps.
  2. Stand all four flaps straight up, open.
  3. Turn it so the short flaps face the arms: one on the robot's left, one on its right. The long flaps are far and near.
  4. Place it in the taped footprint.
Material is a guess

The simulation assumed a crease stiffness and friction, and trained across a range of them. Pre-folded, softer cartons can sag past flat. If you can, measure the crease with a luggage scale and send the reading.

Step 4 · carton
Step 5

Point the cameras the way the policy expects

Owner + agent30 min

Head camera (OAK-D Lite) = "front"

  • Head pan 0°, tilt 58° down.
  • Use the 4:3 full-sensor stream, not 640×360.
  • Check the view shows all four flaps and both claws (top row).
  • Note the head pan/tilt encoder ticks, so the pose can be repeated.

Wrist cameras = "left_wrist", "right_wrist"

  • Mounted as in the robot model, looking along the jaws.
  • The claw tips appear at the bottom of the image (middle and bottom rows).
  • The simulation assumed a 90° field of view. Calibrate the real cameras and send the numbers.

These are the simulated views. Take one still from each real camera with the carton in place and compare.

Step 5 · cameras
Step 6

Measure where the cameras really are

Owner + agent30 min
  1. Before the carton goes down, lay the four tags flat on the platform at taped spots, readable from the robot. Example spots: 120 mm left/right of centre, 250 and 400 mm out from the base line.
  2. Measure each tag's black square, and each tag's spot from the arm bases.
  3. Save one still from each camera.
  4. For each camera, run: 
    python tools/camera_pose_from_tag.py --image <still> \
  --camera front --intrinsics <json> --tag-size <m> \
  --tag ID X Y Z [--tag ...] --update station.json
  5. Read the OAK's factory intrinsics at the 4:3 resolution you use.
No tags? Fallback

Tape-measure the lens position from the arm bases and read the tilt with a phone inclinometer on the camera body. It's good to about ±1 cm and ±1°.

Format of the measurement file: profiles/fold-station-measurement.example.json on branch RonTuretzky/fold-policy-station-gap.

Step 6 · camera poses
Step 7

Send the measurements; the policy is refitted

Agent1–3 h compute

Layout matched (steps 1–4)

Only the cameras changed. The existing demonstrations are re-filmed from the measured camera positions (about 1 minute). Then the dataset is converted, the policy retrained on the cloud GPU (~30–60 min) and re-tested.

Layout differs

Spacing, height or setback can't be matched. New demonstrations are recorded at your measured layout (~20–40 min), then converted and trained the same way.

What you send

  • station.json (measured: true)
  • Photos of each tape measurement
  • One still per camera
  • Head pan/tilt ticks

Step 8 · robot-side gaps
Step 9

Dry run: the policy thinks, the arms don't move

Owner + agentNothing is sent

Arms released and supported, no carton. No --execute, so no command reaches the motors.

python -m carton.fold_policy_runner --checkpoint <ckpt> \
  --transport api --pilot-root "$PILOT" \
  --joint-map left=left-joint-map.json --joint-map right=right-joint-map.json \
  --camera front=<head OAK> --camera left_wrist=<cam> --camera right_wrist=<cam> \
  --out <run dir> --max-steps 100

Check in the log

  • Joint angles match what you measure by hand.
  • No "start outside training" warning. Pose the arms by hand at the training start pose until it clears.
  • Proposed moves are small and sensible.
  • Speed: the effective rate is close to 10 per second.
Step 9 · dry run
Step 10

First motion, then the carton

Owner holds STOP

A · Free air

  1. Enable each arm (it holds in place).
  2. Add --execute --operator <your name> --max-steps 10.
  3. No carton, claws open, nothing in reach.
  4. The run ends holding. Support the arms, then robot_stop.

B · Longer free-air runs

Raise --max-steps only after reading ticks.jsonl from the last run: clamps, warnings, timing.

C · Empty carton

Only after every gap in step 8 is closed and the policy was refitted for your measurements. Start with enough steps for one short flap, STOP in hand, then both flaps.

Step 10 · first motion
Know when it worked, and when to stop

Success looks like

  • The left claw braces the left short flap, the right claw folds the right short flap in, then the left one follows.
  • Both short flaps end flat (about 90°) and are held by the claws.
  • The carton barely moves (the simulation median was 14 mm).
  • About 55 seconds from start to both flaps held.

Press STOP if

  • An arm presses down on the table, the cart or the other arm.
  • The carton slides more than a few centimetres or lifts.
  • A joint stalls or buzzes against a load.
  • Anything moves that you didn't expect.

Write what happened in software/STATUS.md, with the run directory.

Success and stop
One-page checklist

Setup checklist

  • STOP holder and 12 V switch ready
  • Arm spacing measured and photographed
  • Arm bases 120 mm above the carton surface, both sides within 3 mm
  • Cart squared: 111 mm from each pan axis to the table edge
  • Carton footprint taped: near wall 10 mm in, 10 mm left of centre
  • Empty carton, flaps up, short flaps facing the arms
  • Head at pan 0°, tilt 58°, 4:3 stream; ticks noted
  • Wrist cameras see their claw tips
  • Camera poses measured, station.json filled
  • Measurements sent; refitted policy received
  • Joint maps and jaw mapping written
  • Gripper streaming, link speed and joint speed confirmed
  • Dry run clean
  • Free-air 10-step run clean
  • Empty carton, one short flap, then both
