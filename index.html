Status, September 27: no planter parts have been printed and no growing test has happened (the NY K1 Max jammed). The printer now available is an AnkerMake M5C (220 × 220 × 250 mm): the cress planter and the new nest/paddle/bottle-rest parts fit; the square kit's 220 mm outer tray does not. The farm software is written and tested on its simulator; hardware validation starts when the robot is assembled on September 30. Nobody drives the robot: motion is taught by the vision model, decisions go rules → Jev → a person only when needed.

The next three useful actions

  1. Print on the M5C: the cress planter (6 pieces), two nest plates, the light paddle and the bottle rest. About one day of printing.
  2. Flash the ESP32 firmware and run farm sim --auto-answer on the Mac to see the whole program and the viewer before the robot arrives.
  3. September 30: assemble, farm devices → farm calibrate → farm check → farm teach-all → empty-bottle farm once. Build order.
Removed from V0: electric watering pump, external reservoir, hoses/float switches, scale hardware, separate overhead camera and leak pads. The requested silicone mat, adhesive rubber sheets, plant labels and measuring syringes remain out. Use household measuring materials.
