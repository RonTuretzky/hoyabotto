Status, September 26: no planter parts have been printed and no growing test has happened. The K1 Max jammed (PLA in the nozzle; the extruder clicks) after the first job was aborted. Printing resumes once the nozzle is cleared or replaced with a 0.4 mm needle/nozzle kit. The earlier “NY pre-trial” is removed from this guide; the first crop will be sown in Japan or in a household tray if the prints are still missing.

The next three useful actions

  1. Answer the six roadmap decisions, then start Phase 0: adapters, simulator, evidence store and viewer on the Mac.
  2. Clear the K1 Max nozzle (heat to ~220 °C, 0.4 mm needle from below, or swap the nozzle), then print the two kits.
  3. Download the table’s model files. The ZIP received contains Prusa MINI G-code, not the 13 editable 3MF models.
Removed from V0: electric watering pump, external reservoir, hoses/float switches, scale hardware, separate overhead camera and leak pads. The requested silicone mat, adhesive rubber sheets, plant labels and measuring syringes remain out. Use household measuring materials.
