FIELD GUIDE / JAPAN & CARRY-ON

Bring the experiment, not a live crop.

Carry-on only, ANA. Use the New York week for evidence; start a fresh local crop after arrival.

ANA tool restrictions · ANA conditional items / batteries / soldering irons · FAA ordinary dry batteries (including 9V) · MAFF plant quarantine. Recheck the actual itinerary; codeshare and departure-country rules also apply.

Before leaving New York

  1. Unjam the K1 Max and print plate A if time allows; otherwise carry the model files and print at the destination or use a household dish.
  2. Clean and dry any prints; record distortion and snap-fit damage.
  3. Pack model files, the Phase 0 software, ESP32 firmware and notes. Keep data backed up separately.
  4. Arrange the Japan work surface/cart, seeds, basic tools and suitable electrical supplies. Finish modular-table geometry only after the correct files and reach measurements are available.

First Japan session

Confirm delivered kit contents, power-adapter labels and cable types. Follow the numbered robot assembly guide, then add the dry light-sensor bench. Test cameras, serial devices, supply voltages and wiring continuity before wet operation. Then commission dummy trays and very small supervised pours. The first crop is sown here; no growing test preceded it.
